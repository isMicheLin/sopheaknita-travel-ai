Siem Reap Heritage Package, 3 Days and 2 Nights

The sample package includes two hotel nights, daily breakfast, private air-conditioned transport, an English-speaking guide for one full temple day, airport or bus-station pickup, and drinking water during tours.

The suggested itinerary covers Angkor Wat sunrise, Bayon, Ta Prohm, the Old Market area, and an evening free period. The package excludes the Angkor admission pass, lunch, dinner, personal expenses, travel insurance, and optional cultural shows.

The demo price is USD 189 per person based on two travelers sharing one room. Final pricing depends on hotel availability and travel dates.
