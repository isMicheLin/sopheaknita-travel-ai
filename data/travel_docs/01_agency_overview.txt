Sopheaknita Travel Agency Overview

Sopheaknita Travel Agency is a fictional agency created for the CS382 academic demonstration. The agency offers destination guidance, private and shared tours, bus and airport transfers, hotel coordination, and group travel planning within Cambodia.

The demo assistant answers questions from this document collection. All package names, prices, schedules, and policies are sample information. Customers must confirm final details with a human agent before payment or travel.
