Cancellation and Refund Policy

This fictional policy applies to standard demo packages. Cancellation 15 or more days before departure receives an 80 percent refund of amounts paid, excluding non-refundable supplier fees. Cancellation 7 to 14 days before departure receives a 50 percent refund, excluding non-refundable supplier fees. Cancellation fewer than 7 days before departure is non-refundable.

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7 to 14 business days after all required documents are received. Supplier-specific tickets, promotional fares, and special-event bookings may have stricter terms shown in the quotation.
