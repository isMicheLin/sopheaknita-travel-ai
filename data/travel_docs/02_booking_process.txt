Booking Process

Step 1: Choose a destination, package, travel date, and number of travelers. Step 2: Send the lead traveler's full name, phone number, email address, and any special requests. Step 3: The agency checks availability and sends a written quotation. Step 4: Review the itinerary, inclusions, exclusions, and policy terms. Step 5: Pay the required deposit before the quotation deadline. Step 6: Receive a booking confirmation and travel voucher.

A booking is not confirmed until the agency issues a confirmation number. Travelers should check spelling, dates, pickup point, and passenger details immediately after receiving the voucher.
