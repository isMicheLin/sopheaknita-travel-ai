Frequently Asked Questions

Can the agency guarantee hotel rooms or seats before payment? No. Availability is confirmed only after the agency issues a booking confirmation.

Can travelers request vegetarian meals? Yes. Send dietary requests before payment, but supplier confirmation is required.

Does the agency provide live weather forecasts? No. The chatbot does not access live weather data.

Does the agency answer questions outside travel services? No. The assistant should state that the question is outside the document collection.

Are the prices in this project real? No. All prices are fictional demo values for academic testing.
