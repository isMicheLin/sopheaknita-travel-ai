Cardamom Mountain Trek, 3 Days and 2 Nights

This sample adventure includes local transfers from the meeting point, two basic lodge or homestay nights, a trained local guide, listed meals, drinking water, and community conservation fees.

Participants should be able to walk on uneven and muddy trails for several hours. The trip is not suitable for travelers who need step-free access. Personal equipment, insurance, tips, and transport to the meeting point are excluded. The demo price is USD 220 per person.
