Kep Seafood Day Trip

The sample day trip departs Phnom Penh at 6:30 AM and returns around 8:00 PM. It includes shared transport, a local coordinator, bottled water, a stop at the crab market, free time near Kep Beach, and a short visit to a nearby viewpoint.

Seafood meals, island boats, insurance, and personal expenses are excluded. The demo price is USD 35 per person with a minimum of six travelers.
